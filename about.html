About Us

Tihar Bazaar — Kathmandu, Nepal

Tihar Bazaar is an online store based in Kathmandu, Nepal. We sell festive essentials — decorations, diyas, pooja items, clothing — and electronics, delivered across Nepal.

Our promise

Direct from the supplier. No middlemen. We add only a 10% margin on the supplier's rate, so you get the lowest honest price.

No advance payment. You pay only when you receive your items (Cash on Delivery).

How ordering works

  1. You order — choose products on this website or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. We forward it — your order goes straight to the supplier.
  3. Supplier ships directly — items come to your address without passing through us.
  4. You receive & pay — check your items at delivery and pay then.
