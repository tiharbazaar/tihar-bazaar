Shipping Policy

Simple, honest delivery across Nepal.

🚚 Delivery area: All of Nepal

⏱️ Estimated time: 3–5 days after order confirmation

💵 Payment: Cash on Delivery — pay when your items arrive

How shipping works

  • Your order is forwarded to the supplier, who ships directly to your address.
  • The delivery charge is told to you when the order is confirmed — before anything ships. No hidden charges, no surprises.
  • Delivery time may vary slightly by location (remote areas can take a little longer).

Tracking your order

Message us on WhatsApp at +977 9719631185 with your name or order details, and we will tell you where your items are.

What if nobody is home?

The delivery person will usually try again or call you. To avoid delays, please share a correct address and a reachable phone number when ordering.
