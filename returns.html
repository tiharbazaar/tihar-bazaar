Return & Refund Policy

If something is wrong, we make it right.

↩️ Wrong or damaged item? Message us on WhatsApp with a photo of the item as soon as you receive it — we will arrange a replacement or refund.

How to request a return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at +977 9719631185.
  2. Send a photo of the wrong or damaged item, plus your name and order details.
  3. We will confirm the issue and arrange a replacement or refund — whichever you prefer.

Refunds

  • We take no advance payment — you pay Cash on Delivery. So in most cases there is nothing to refund: if an item is wrong or damaged, you can simply refuse it at delivery and pay nothing.
  • If you already paid and the item qualifies for return, the refund is arranged directly with you on WhatsApp.

What is not covered

  • Change of mind after delivery (the product is correct and undamaged).
  • Damage caused by misuse after delivery.
  • Slight colour or packaging differences from product photos (see Terms & Conditions).

Need help?

Talk to us directly — a real person will help you.
